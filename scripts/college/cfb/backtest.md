# CFB model backtest (walk-forward, closing lines)

Generated by `backtest.py`. Data: ESPN scoreboards (FBS + FCS results) and ESPN core-API closing lines (2026: DraftKings; 2025: ESPN BET, a few DraftKings). Bets graded at -110 (breakeven 52.38%). Win% CIs are Wilson 95%; ROI CIs are normal-approx 95% on per-bet returns. Pushes count as 0 return.

## Method

* **Margin model**: ridge regression, `margin_home = HFA*(not neutral) + r_home - r_away`, margins capped; team rating = group offset (FBS=0 / FCS / other, weakly penalized) + team deviation shrunk toward `rho * previous-season deviation`. FCS teams get their own ratings (FCS-vs-FCS games are included) pooled toward a fitted FCS mean.
* **Points model (totals)**: `pts = mu + HP*home + off_team - def_opp`, same shrinkage scheme, two rows per game.
* **No look-ahead**: for each week w, fit only on that season's games with week < w (+ prior-season ratings).
* **Hyperparameters** tuned on **2025 only** (MAE vs actual, weeks 4-15). Margin: `{'lam': 2.0, 'rho': 0.8, 'cap': 0, 'half_life': None}` (MAE 12.15). Points: `{'lam_pts': 8.0, 'rho_pts': 0.4, 'pts_cap': 63.0, 'half_life': None}` (MAE 12.33).
* **Rule selection**: threshold x segment picked on 2025 weeks 5-15 (best ROI, n>=60), then frozen and graded on 2026 weeks 5-13. Second split: pick on 2026 wk 5-8, grade wk 9-13.
