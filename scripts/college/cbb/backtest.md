## Verdict

- spread: threshold ≥2, out-of-sample 107-112-0, ROI -6.7% [-19.2, +5.8] → **no edge**
- total: threshold ≥8, out-of-sample 35-24-0, ROI +13.3% [-11.0, +35.4] → **weak edge candidate (OOS ROI > 0 but CI includes losing)**

Rule for flagging picks in today.md: out-of-sample ROI > 0 on ≥40 bets at the threshold chosen on the tuning half ("weak" if the 95% CI still includes negative ROI, "proven" only if the whole CI is above 0). A stricter bar drafted earlier (CI lower bound > −10%) is *not* met by either market.

### Caveats

- Two weeks of out-of-sample data; every CI above is wide enough to include both a sizeable edge and a sizeable loss.
- Totals: model − line regressed on actual − line gives a positive slope in both halves (table in section c; significant in the tuning half and pooled, not on its own in the test half), but the test-half win came mostly from *overs* during a stretch when games went over the closing total on average; the tuning half was balanced over/under.
- Spreads: the model is less accurate than the closing spread and shows no ATS signal at any threshold.
- Lines are DraftKings closing numbers via ESPN; bets placed earlier at worse/better numbers will differ.
